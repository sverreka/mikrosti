Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Short version: You can use MikroSti without an account, and then nothing about you is stored anywhere except in your own browser. If you create an account, we store your email, the name you chose and your progress so you can continue on other devices. We never sell data, show ads or use tracking cookies. You can delete your account and all its data yourself at any time.

Who is responsible

MikroSti is run by Sverre Kaldestad, who is the data controller. Questions or requests: sverrekaldestad12@gmail.com.

If you log in with Google, we receive your email address and name from Google. Nothing else.

Without an account

Your progress is saved only in your browser’s local storage on your device. It is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase stores accounts, progress, the leaderboard and reports.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, it sees your IP address when you load the page and may keep it in logs for security.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the fonts and the login library, so they also see your IP address when the page loads.
  • Google handles the login if you choose “Continue with Google”.

Some of these companies are based in the USA. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Cookies and local storage

We use no tracking or advertising cookies. The site uses your browser’s local storage to keep you logged in and to save progress. This is strictly necessary for the site to work, so no cookie banner is needed.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Leaderboard entries are deleted when you leave the leaderboard or delete your account. Problem reports are deleted once the problem is handled, and at the latest after 12 months.

Your rights

  • Delete everything: log in, click your initial in the top right corner, and choose “Delete account”. Your account, progress and leaderboard entry are deleted right away.
  • Access, correction or a copy of your data: email us and we will reply within 30 days.
  • Complain: you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).
